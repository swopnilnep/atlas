Atlas

Observed geography of real places

Atlas is an open-source generative cartography project. It turns real terrain, streets, water, parks, and other geographic data into downloadable artwork.

Each piece starts from measured data: elevation from satellite and survey models, and roads, rivers, and coastlines mapped by OpenStreetMap contributors. The images are generated live in your browser when you load or download them. The aim is to show what makes a place itself: the river a city bends around, the ice that carved a valley, the delta a river builds where it meets the sea.
